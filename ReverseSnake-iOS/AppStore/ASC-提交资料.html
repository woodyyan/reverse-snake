HOW TO PLAY

审核备注 Notes（英文，直接粘贴）
Snake Feast is a single-player arcade game. No account or login is required; all features are available immediately after launch.

How to play: tap "Start Game", then swipe on the board to change direction and tap the board to dash. Eat golden seeds to score; the run ends when the player touches the snake.

Game Center is optional. If the device is signed in to Game Center, scores are submitted to two leaderboards (snakefeast.best.alltime, snakefeast.best.weekly), viewable via the "Leaderboard" button on the home screen or in the pause menu. If not signed in, the game is fully playable offline with a local best score.

The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party SDKs, and it does not collect any user data.

Snake Feast（以下简称“本游戏”）由 【开发者法定名称】（以下简称“我们”）开发并发布。我们非常重视你的隐私，本政策说明本游戏如何处理信息。

1. 我们不收集个人数据

本游戏不要求注册或登录，不包含任何广告、统计分析或第三方 SDK，我们没有自建服务器，不会收集、存储、出售或共享你的任何个人数据，也不会进行跨 App 追踪。

2. 仅保存在你设备上的数据

  • 最高分、音效开关、外观（浅色/深色）设置；
  • 尚未同步到 Game Center 的成绩（联网后自动提交并清除）。

以上数据仅保存在你的设备本地，删除本游戏即会一并清除。

3. Game Center（可选）

如果你的设备已登录 Apple Game Center，本游戏会把你的游戏得分提交到 Game Center 排行榜，并展示排行榜中的玩家昵称、头像与分数。上述数据由 Apple 依据Apple 隐私政策处理，我们无法访问你的 Apple 账户信息。你可以随时在“设置 › Game Center”中退出登录，退出后本游戏仍可完整离线游玩。

4. 儿童隐私

本游戏适合所有年龄段，不会有意收集任何儿童的个人信息。

5. 政策更新

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

6. 联系我们

Snake Feast (the “Game”) is developed and published by [Developer legal name] (“we”). This policy explains how the Game handles information.

1. We do not collect personal data

The Game requires no account, contains no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs, and we operate no servers. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Your best score, sound and appearance (light/dark) settings;
  • Scores not yet synced to Game Center (submitted and cleared automatically once you are online).

This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the Game.

3. Game Center (optional)

If your device is signed in to Apple Game Center, the Game submits your scores to Game Center leaderboards and displays player nicknames, avatars and scores from those leaderboards. This data is processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; we have no access to your Apple Account. You can sign out anytime in Settings › Game Center and keep playing fully offline.

4. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We will update this page and its effective date if this policy changes.

常见问题 · FAQ

怎么玩？ 在棋盘上滑动转向，轻点棋盘冲刺 2 格（冷却 2.6 秒）。吃金种 +1 分，蛇会变长变快；碰到蛇身或被蛇头追上即结束，撞墙不会输。
How to play: swipe to turn, tap to dash 2 cells (2.6s cooldown). Each golden seed is +1 point and makes the snake longer and faster. Touching the snake ends the run; walls only block you.

排行榜没有显示 / 成绩没上传？ 请在“设置 › Game Center”登录后回到游戏；离线时的成绩会在联网后自动补交。
Leaderboard not showing? Sign in at Settings › Game Center. Offline scores are submitted automatically when you're back online.

最高分会丢失吗？ 最高分保存在本机，删除 App 会清除本机记录；已提交到 Game Center 的成绩不受影响。
Your best score is stored on device; deleting the app clears it, but scores already on Game Center remain.

如何切换浅色 / 深色？ 主页或暂停面板中的“外观”，可选跟随系统、浅色或深色。
Switch themes from Appearance on the home screen or pause menu.
